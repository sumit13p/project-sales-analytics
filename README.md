# Project Sales Analytics

## Project Overview
An end-to-end sales analytics portfolio project using a retail sales dataset. The objective is to transform raw sales data into validated business insights through Python, SQL, and business intelligence.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze annual sales performance and year-over-year changes.
- Identify sales growth and decline across different time periods.
- Investigate regional, category, sub-category, and customer sales patterns.
- Validate analytical results and communicate actionable business findings.

## Dataset
- **Records:** 9,800
- **Columns:** 20, including derived analytical columns
- **Date coverage:** 2015–2018
- **Primary metric:** Sales

The dataset includes order dates, shipping dates, customer details, product information, geography, and sales values. The raw dataset is not included in this repository.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- SQL (SQLite)
- Google Colab
- GitHub

Power BI, forecasting, PySpark, and Snowflake are planned extensions and will be documented as they are implemented.

## Analysis Completed
- Data quality checks and missing-value assessment
- Shipping-duration analysis
- Annual sales and year-over-year growth analysis
- Monthly sales comparisons
- Regional, category, sub-category, and customer analysis
- Validation and reconciliation of sales changes

## Key Findings
- Annual sales increased from approximately $479,856 in 2015 to $722,052 in 2018.
- Sales declined in 2016 before increasing in 2017 and 2018.
- Sales in 2018 increased by approximately $121,859 compared with 2017.
- November 2018 was an important contributor to the annual sales increase.
